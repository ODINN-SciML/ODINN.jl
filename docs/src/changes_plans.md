# Ongoing changes and future plans

In this page we will attempt to document the main ongoing changes in terms of model development, and the main features we are planning for the future.

  - Changes in transient inversions that modify existing results:

      + Each observation now counts once, with the same weight (`weighting = :uniform`). Before, an observation was weighted by the time since the previous one, and the first one was ignored. Loss values and the weights `λ` tuned before change. Since the loss is larger, the first step of a plain `LBFGS()` is larger too, and can put a bounded parameter on its bound, where LBFGS stops: use the LBFGS of `ODINN.default_optimizer()`.
      + `θ.IC` holds, for each glacier, the fields `H` (the initial thickness divided by `scale_H`, by default the maximum initial thickness, `H₀_scale`) and, when the bed is free, `S`. Use `ODINN.initial_thickness(θ, simulation, id)` to get metres. A `θ.IC` saved by an older version cannot be read directly.
      + All the terms of the loss are a mean of `(misfit / σ)²` (data) or `(|D a| / σ)²` (regularization), so they are about 1 when the model is as far as the uncertainty, at any resolution, and `λ` is 1 by default. Loss values and the weights tuned before change. `L2Sum` and `LogSum` lose `normalize`, `LossV` loses `scale_loss`, and the data losses get `σ` (by default the uncertainty of the data: per cell for the GlaThiDa surveys). `TikhonovRegularization` uses the gradient by default (the Laplacian with `operator = :laplacian`), with a `σ`, and a mean over the glacier. `run!` prints the value of each term, see `loss_terms`.
      + `InitialCondition` inverts the bed by default (`bed = :free`), which needs `SciMLSensitivityAdjoint` and a `LossS`. To invert the initial thickness only, or with the manual adjoints, use `InitialCondition(...; bed = :fixed)`.
      + The outline of the glacier is a hard constraint of the initial thickness. The outline is where `glacier.H₀` is not zero (the glacier at the date of the DEM). Outside it `H₀` is zero, also in the zone around the outline where the glacier may advance, and inside it `H₀` is at least `min_thickness` of `InitialCondition` (1 m by default; use `0.0` for no minimum). Before, the inversion could put ice in the zone around the outline, since with a free bed it does not change the surface and no data are there. `S₀` is still free in that zone. `evaluate_H₀` and `evaluate_∂H₀` have the keyword `H_min`, and `ic_scales` has the field `Hmin`.
      + The default optimizer is Adam and then LBFGS (`default_optimizer()`), with 25 epochs each. With a vector of optimizers, `epochs` needs one number per optimizer.
      + The surface velocity includes the sliding term of the flux (plug flow) and is placed on the grid of `H`, so the velocity losses change when `C > 0`.
      + `InitialThicknessRegularization()` applies at the start of each glacier. A `t₀` outside the time window of a glacier is an error.
      + With an ice thickness survey at most one mass balance step after the start, an inversion of the initial condition starts on the survey (`align_start!`).
      + With `use_glathida_data = true`, the dated Glathida campaigns are attached as `thicknessData`, so `LossH` uses them. These surveys are sparse: use a loss with `distance = 0`, for example `L2Sum(distance = 0)`, or the mask removes them.
      + The sign of the Millan22 `vy` is now that of the model `Vy`, so velocity losses with `component = :xy` change.
      + Huginn: `surface_V!` is removed, use `surface_V` or `surface_V_inplace!`. Muninn 0.16: the mass balance throws `Muninn.ElevationOutOfRange` and not a `DomainError` when the surface leaves the range of the table.
      + `TikhonovRegularization` has the fields `operator::Symbol`, `σ` and `distance` (`distance` is not used). The fields `operator_forward` and `operator_reverse` are removed.
      + `evaluate_H₀` and `evaluate_∂H₀` have the keyword `scale`, the scale of `θ.IC`. Its default is `H₀_scale(glacier)`, which is right only when `scale_H` of `InitialCondition` is not set: use `ODINN.initial_thickness`.
      + `LossDhdt` is divided by `σ²`, with σ from the uncertainty of the data (by default 0.2 m/yr), and `LossAvgV` too (by default 10 m/yr).
      + `create_results` has the keyword `B`, the bed of the results (by default `glacier.B`).
      + Gungnir writes the Millan22 velocity with its error bands (`add_error = True`: `millan_err_vx`, `millan_err_vy`). When they are in `gridded_data.nc`, the velocity losses use the mean error of the map as σ (the size of the error vector divided by √2, for `:xy` and `:abs`), and 10 m/yr when the file has no error bands. Files made before must be processed again, or get the bands with OGGM's `millan_velocity_to_gdir(gdir, add_error=True)`.
      + The manual adjoints of the velocity losses were updated for the new sliding term of the velocity for `SIA2D_A_target` and `SIA2D_D_target`. The one of `SIA2D_D_hybrid_target` was not (it is not tested with a velocity loss and a sliding coefficient): use `SciMLSensitivityAdjoint` for it.
      + `Huginn.Halfar_velocity` throws an `ArgumentError` when the Halfar parameters have a sliding fraction `f ≠ 0`, since the formula has no sliding.
      + With `SciMLSensitivityAdjoint` and several glaciers, the gradient of each glacier is put at its own place in `θ` (it was added to the one of the first glacier before).
      + Old saved data cannot be read: `ThicknessData` has the new field `σ`, `Glacier2D` has the new field `surfaceData` (with a new type parameter), and `SimulationParameters` has `glacier_tspans`. Saved `.jld2` files of glaciers and parameters must be created again.

  - The bed can be inverted with the initial geometry, as in IGM (`InitialCondition` with `bed = :free`: the unknowns are `H₀` and `S₀`, and the bed is `S₀ - H₀`), with dated DEMs as observations of the surface (`LossS`, `glacier.surfaceData`, see [Inversion of the bed](@ref)) and a prior on the whole bed (`BedRegularization`). For now only `SciMLSensitivityAdjoint` supports it; the manual adjoints refuse a free bed. The code that reads `glacier.B` directly still uses the bed of the glacier and not the inverted one: the laws whose input is the bed (`iTopoRough` with `position = :bed`) and the interpolation nodes of the `U` law.

  - Later: learn the σ of each term, the σ per pixel of the velocity maps, a bias per DEM, and remove the unused argument `normalization` of the losses.

  - End-to-end differentiability of the whole ODINN ecosystem with `SciMLSensitivity.jl` and `Enzyme.jl` is now available through `SciMLSensitivityAdjoint`, alongside ODINN's own manual adjoints. We are continuing to broaden the set of targets and losses covered by the automatic path, and to consolidate it across the ecosystem.

  - We plan to add the Depth-Integrated Viscosity Approximation (DIVA) to `Huginn` and `ODINN` in the coming months, to improve on the physics of the Shallow Ice Approximation. Unlike the SIA, DIVA accounts for longitudinal and lateral stresses and resolves the velocity as part of the solution, which matters for fast-flowing and sliding-dominated glaciers. It will be usable with both the forward and the inverse capabilities of ODINN.

  - We are interested in implementing other glacier equations inside ODINN (e.g., full Stokes equation, Shallow Shelf Equation). ODINN offers large flexibility and composability to easily integrate new differential equations that can utilize both the forward and inverse capabilities inside ODINN.

  - GPU compatibility is still not available. For now, we are focusing on having everything parallelized with multiprocessing while being compatible with automatic differentiation (AD). Once this codebase is stable, we might implement a GPU-compatible version.
